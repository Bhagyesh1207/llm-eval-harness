How the judge works

Faithfulness. An NLI model (nli-deberta-v3-xsmall) checks whether the passage entails the answer. Small NLI models read short premises better than long ones, so the answer is checked against the whole passage, each sentence, and each pair of neighbouring sentences, and the best score counts. An answer is faithful when entailment is at least 0.5.

Correctness. For questions that have an answer, the reply must contain most of the words of a gold answer from SQuAD v2. For questions with no answer in the passage, the model must say so.

Hallucination. The model gave an answer that the passage does not back up, or answered a question the passage cannot answer.

Relevance. Cosine similarity between question and answer embeddings (all-MiniLM-L6-v2).
